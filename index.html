Step by step

How to download in three steps

The same flow works for every supported platform, whether you want the full video or just the audio track.

Step 01

Copy the link

Open the video or post on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn and copy its share link.

Step 02

Paste and grab

Paste it into the box above and press Grab it. Grabsy reads the link and lists every quality available.

Step 03

Choose a format

Pick 1080p, 720p, 480p or MP3 audio, and the file saves straight to your device.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Grabsy free to use?

Yes. Pasting a link and downloading a video or MP3 doesn't require an account, a subscription or an app install.

Is it legal to download videos this way?

Downloading is intended for content you own, have explicit permission to save, or that's shared under a license permitting downloads. You're responsible for how you use downloaded files and for following each platform's terms.

Why does TikTok save without a watermark?

Grabsy fetches the original render of the clip rather than the shared re-encode, so the on-screen username sticker used for shared links never gets baked in.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The page is fully responsive and works in any modern mobile browser on iOS or Android, no app required.

Which audio format do I get from MP3 downloads?

Audio-only downloads are delivered as standard MP3 files, so they play on virtually any device or media player.

Is there a daily download limit?

Reasonable personal use is unrestricted. Automated or bulk scraping is not permitted — see our Terms of Service for details.
